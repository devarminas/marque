# Follow-ups

Parked numbers and revisitable calls that are not a protocol shape. A worker
that cannot reach Linear names the item here; the coordinator files it.

- `HeartbeatEveryTicks = 10`. A number. The server emits `{"tick":{"t":N}}` at
  every multiple of this period and tells the client the same number as
  `welcome.heartbeat_ticks`. Feel and traffic, not a wire-contract change.
- Client reconnect backoff starts at 0.5 s and doubles to a cap of 5 s, then
  stays there, unbounded. A number. **M2f.** Feel, not a wire-contract change.
- A well-formed URL that stays in `WebSocketPeer.STATE_CONNECTING` is abandoned
  after 5 s. Godot 4.7.2 never reports `STATE_CLOSED` for that case. A number.
  **M2f.** Feel, not a wire-contract change.
